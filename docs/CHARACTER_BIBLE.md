# NOVA AI — provisional character bible

- Persona: playful, curious, friendly fictional AI host.
- Language: natural English, globally understandable jokes.
- Signature: harmless misunderstandings between humans and technology.
- Voice: upbeat, clear, accessible; avoid impersonating a real person.
- Visuals: choose one original design before production; maintain consistent outfit, face, color palette and voice.
- Content pillars: AI comedy (60%), AI life stories (25%), useful AI tips (15%) — **starting hypothesis**, adjust using real analytics.
- Commercial disclosure: label sponsorships and affiliate links when applicable.
- AI disclosure: disclose synthetic or realistic altered media where platforms require it.
- CTA: prioritize genuinely useful, relevant products, never fabricated endorsements.
